Scope Boundaries & Constraints

Section 2.1 - 2.2

In Scope (MVP v1.0)

  • Real-time human pose estimation & landmark tracking
  • Rep counting, set tracking & posture error scoring
  • Muscle fatigue scoring & recovery estimation
  • Local LLM adaptive workout planning & conversational chat
  • Human-in-the-Loop Trainer Review workflow

Out of Scope (Explicit Promise)

  • Medical disease diagnosis or physical therapy treatment
  • Wearable hardware integration (Smartwatch / HR sensors)
  • In-app payments, subscriptions, or financial transactions
  • Social feeds, community posts, or public marketplaces
Privacy Constraint Raw camera frames are processed in-memory and NEVER stored or transmitted off-device.
Latency Constraint Pose estimation & form analysis execute locally under strict near-real-time latency limits.

Core Functional Architecture (FR-1 to FR-6)

Computer Vision Core
FR-2

Pose Landmarks

Captures video frames, extracts key body joints continuous tracking, and alerts on low lighting or occlusion.

FR-3

Rep & Set Counting

Automatically identifies exercise state transitions, counting accurate reps while ignoring non-workout movement.

FR-4

Form Analysis

Generates real-time Form Score, error tags (e.g. knee caving), and under 15-word corrective audio cues.

FR-5

Muscle Workload

Estimates primary/secondary muscle activation per exercise (e.g., Quadriceps: High, Gluteus: Moderate).

FR-6

Fatigue & Recovery

Categorizes muscle groups into recovery states (High Fatigue -> Rest) to prevent overtraining syndrome.

FR-9

Calorie & Hydration

Calculates energy output and hydration needs via deterministic engines, feeding verified metrics to LLM.

All exercise form and pose classification feedback runs locally on-device to maintain low latency.

Technical Risks & Architecture Trade-offs

Feedback Topic A
Risk 1: On-Device LLM Constraints

Mobile RAM & Battery Consumption

Running quantized Local LLMs alongside real-time MediaPipe/YOLO pose tracking may cause thermal throttling on lower-end devices.

Proposed Mitigation: Fallback to lightweight rules-engine or server-side hybrid LLM inference when device battery/RAM is low.
Risk 2: Occlusion & Low Light

Pose Tracking Accuracy Degradation

User position relative to phone camera (loose clothing, tight bedroom spaces) affects joint coordinate accuracy.

Proposed Mitigation: Visual bounds checking on UI during setup + strict pose confidence scoring threshold before evaluating form errors.
Risk 3: Safety Escalation Threshold

False Positives in Human Escalation

Over-flagging safe routines to Human Trainers could overwhelm human reviewers, causing bottlenecks in workout approval.

Proposed Mitigation: Calibrated risk classification model with tiered triggers (Low Risk = Direct AI execution, High Risk = Trainer Review).
Area for Advisor Guidance: How strict should the Safety Layer boundary be between automated AI vs. Mandatory Trainer Escalation?
